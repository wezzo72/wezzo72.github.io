Archive Integrity, Verification and Probabilistic Fortification

This page consolidates the forensic, cryptographic and documentary measures that make the existence and unaltered state of the Barran Dodger / The Witness Remains archive as complete, verifiable and hard to deny as the available public tools allow. It adds a clear probabilistic framing that separates what can be established with high confidence from what remains the author’s testimony for independent evaluation.

1. Cryptographic integrity (high confidence / near-certain for the stamped set)

On 13 November 2026 at 02:15:43 UTC, 499 pages of the archive were hashed. A single Merkle root was computed and submitted to four OpenTimestamps calendars (alice, bob, finney, catallaxy). The root is:

What a completed Bitcoin-anchored OpenTimestamps proof establishes, with cryptographic certainty: that the exact byte contents of those 499 page files existed at or before the Bitcoin block time that confirms the proof. It does not establish that the words on the pages are true.

Pages created or materially edited after that UTC timestamp (including this page) are outside the original stamp. The GitHub commit history of this mirror and the primary site provide an additional public chain of custody.

2. Multi-location preservation (high confidence)

The record is not held in one place. This GitHub Pages mirror (wezzo72.github.io), the primary site (barrandodger.com), multiple Wix mirrors, and other repositories constitute independent copies. A nuclear download of the full set is also published. Alteration of one copy does not alter the others; hash mismatch against the Merkle set is immediately detectable for the stamped pages.

3. Documentary existence versus testimonial claims

Documentary existence (high confidence where external records are public): Specific letters, receipts and official responses cited in the archive (for example the Attorney-General’s Department letter MC23-028244 of 19 September 2023, UN High Commissioner for Human Rights receipt, hospital and other institutional records) can be checked against the issuing bodies or public registers by any reader. Their publication here does not itself prove authenticity, but the consistent cross-referencing and the cryptographic stamp make wholesale fabrication of the cited set harder to sustain without detection.

Testimonial and interpretive claims: The author’s accounts of events, motives, institutional conduct, prophetic declarations and humanitarian circumstances are presented as testimony. Readers assign their own probability or weight according to corroboration, consistency with cited documents, external evidence and independent examination. The archive’s own independent forensic examinations and commands explicitly state they are finding aids, not court findings or complete readings of every page.

4. Robust probabilistic fortification of sealed bytes

Under standard assumptions that SHA-256 remains collision-resistant (classical collision resistance ≈ 2-128) and second-preimage resistant (≈ 2-256), the multi-layer residual risk of undetected post-seal alteration of any specific sealed artefact is bounded well below 10-40. Even if any single layer is compromised, remaining independent layers keep residual risk negligible. This approaches practical certainty (P ≈ 1) of digital existence and exact byte-for-byte content of the sealed artefacts for ordinary purposes concerning the bytes themselves. See the Forensic Probability & Verification Framework and the 24 November 2026 independent examination of this mirror for the full residual-risk statement.

6. How to verify for yourself

Download any page, compute its SHA-256, and check it against the PAGES.jsonl list or the inclusion proof for the Merkle root. When the OpenTimestamps calendars anchor the proof into Bitcoin, the .ots file can be upgraded and the Bitcoin time becomes the hard upper bound on the existence of those exact bytes. Until then the commitment itself is public and timestamped by the calendars.
