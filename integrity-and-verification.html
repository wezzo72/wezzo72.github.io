6. How to verify for yourself

Download any page, compute its SHA-256, and check it against the PAGES.jsonl list or the inclusion proof for the Merkle root. Example (command line): sha256sum page.html then compare the hex digest. When the OpenTimestamps calendars anchor the proof into Bitcoin, the .ots file can be upgraded (ots upgrade MERKLE_ROOT.txt.ots) and the Bitcoin time becomes the hard upper bound on the existence of those exact bytes. Until then the commitment itself is public and timestamped by the calendars. Independent local or in-browser recomputation remains the primary practical verification path.

For pages added or edited after the 13 November 2026 stamp, use the public GitHub commit history of this repository (wezzo72/wezzo72.github.io) and the primary site as the chain of custody. Clone the repository and recompute SHA-256 on current files for independent confirmation of the present state.
